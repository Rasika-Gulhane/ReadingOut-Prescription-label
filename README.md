Empowering accessibility and independence in healthcare, our project integrates advanced OCR technology with LLM (Langchain) sequence model Touch for accurate perfromancecand text-to-speech synthesis to provide a seamless solution for elderly and visually impaired individuals, ensuring accurate and audible information extraction from prescription documents.
